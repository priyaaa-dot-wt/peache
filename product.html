dry_cleaning How to Care for Your Bag
expand_more

Take care of your bag and it will last:

  • Keep perfume and hairspray away from the beads. Put perfume on first.
  • Keep it dry. Wipe gently with a soft cloth.
  • Store it in its dust bag.
  • Do not hang it by the handle for a long time.
